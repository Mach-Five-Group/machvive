Difference

MachVive is different. It's a framework that doesn't get in the way. It's unobtrusive and highly configurable. It's deployed for interactive chat experiences.

Performance

Built with vanilla JavaScript and CSS, MachVive prioritizes speed. MachVive is fast, responsive, low-code, analytics friendly, modular, and secure. It doesn't break. It's opinionated by design to handle thousands of concurrent business experiences.

Simplicity

MachVive is the anti-framework. Its intuitive developer experience without sacrificing power. Its intuitive developer experience with AI based agility without sacrificing power. Simple > complex.

Acceleration

Designed for the modern attention economy, MachVive acts as a rheostat of accelerometer aligned digital touchpoints within CIAM.

Declarative

Built with vanilla JavaScript and an HTML-first approach, MachVive is fast, responsive, low-code, analytics friendly, accessible, and designed to scale. It's opinionated by design to handle complex UX. declarative > imperative.

Plug-in

MachVive is the anti-framework for developers who want simplicity without sacrificing ability to extend functionality experience with AI based experience data in JSON2.
